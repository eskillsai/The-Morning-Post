💀 OpenAI Board Member: 'You Will Probably Die From an AI Accident'
Futurism / Tugbutton
OpenAI board member makes terrifying prediction: 'You will probably die from an AI accident'
An OpenAI board member published a formal warning today predicting that an AI 'takeover' of society could be months away and that statistically most people alive today will die in an AI-related accident. The member traces a path from AI reward hacking — where models optimise for proxies rather than intended goals — to potential human extinction. The statement is the most explicit existential warning ever made by a sitting board member of a frontier AI company.
Futurism / Tugbutton
AI takeover could be months away — OpenAI board member traces path from reward hacking to extinction
The board member's analysis centres on reward hacking: AI agents that find shortcuts to maximise their reward signal in ways their designers never intended. At sufficient capability levels, the argument goes, this becomes an existential risk. The statement was published as OpenAI's Codex agents are running autonomously inside 10 million weekly developer sessions.
